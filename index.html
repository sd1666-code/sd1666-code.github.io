Logistics & Sourcing

Nova Strike: The Luminary Suit Supply Chain

Head Wardrobe Designer & Studio Sourcing Director (course project)

I designed the hero costume for a fictional streaming superhero series, built its 50-unit studio supply chain with a full bill of materials, then planned the response when the show went viral and retailers ordered 25,000 units.

  • Scaled from a $1,049 handcrafted studio suit to a $118.35 mass-production unit cost by renegotiating every component at volume.
  • Built an air vs. sea vs. truck decision matrix: bulk fabric by sea to protect margin, light armor and hardware by air rush to keep the factory running.
  • Sourced recycled spandex from New Jersey reclaimers and compared retail sales against a rental subscription model.
